## About

Cybersecurity Analyst focused on operational Threat Intelligence and honeypot operations. I run my own multi-service honeypot infrastructure on a public VPS (SSH, Web, Redis, Docker, PostgreSQL) and contribute captured samples and IOCs to the public threat intelligence ecosystem.

- First submitter worldwide of novel samples from the Outlaw/Shellbot kit on MalwareBazaar.
- Continuous IOC reporting to ThreatFox covering active malware families: `elf.perlbot`, `elf.malxmr`, `elf.dofloo`, RedTail, Mirai.
- Submitted samples independently validated by the THOR APT Scanner (Florian Roth, Nextron Systems).
- Master's in Cybersecurity in progress. Background in Systems Administration (ASIR).
